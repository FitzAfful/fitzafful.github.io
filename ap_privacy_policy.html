Privacy Policy

Last updated: January 2026

1. Overview

ProdInsight (“the App”) is designed to help developers view and inspect analytics events from their own applications. We take privacy seriously and intentionally collect as little data as possible.

2. Information We Collect

We do not require user accounts.

We do not collect personal data.

The App does not collect:

  • names, email addresses, or identifiers
  • analytics data from your users
  • tracking data for advertising or profiling

3. Analytics Event Data

When you connect a third-party analytics provider (such as Mixpanel), the App retrieves event data only on your device.

Analytics event data:

  • is fetched on demand
  • is cached temporarily in memory
  • is never stored on our servers
  • is never shared with third parties

4. Credentials & Security

Third-party API credentials are stored securely in the device Keychain. They never leave your device and are never transmitted to us.

5. iCloud Sync

If enabled, project metadata (such as project names and preferences) may be synced via iCloud between your devices.

Analytics event data is never synced or stored long-term.

6. In-App Purchases

Purchases are handled by the Apple App Store. We do not receive or store payment information.

7. Children’s Privacy

ProdInsight is a developer tool and is not intended for children under 13.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Continued use of the App constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
